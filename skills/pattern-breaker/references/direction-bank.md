# Direction bank: 20 design worlds from remarkable sites

Read this in the concept lab and in upgrade directions (U7), next to `idea-bank.md`. A **direction** is a whole world: a place or object the page becomes, with its own colour, type, button and one signature scroll. Each entry names the real site it comes from (all checked 2026-10-13), what the scroll does there, and how to translate it. Copying the world is allowed; copying the surface is not: re-derive palette and type from the client's own material (reflexes.md).

How to read an entry: **World** (what the page becomes) · **Seen in** (the real site) · **The scroll** (the experience) · **Build** (cost and the light way to do it) · **Translate** (what to take from the client) · **MediaWave** (an example translation, from the October 2026 board).

Cost: *light* = CSS and small JS; *medium* = one engine scene, SVG or CSS 3D; *heavy* = WebGL, many assets. Heavy worlds need the light version first (most of the wow comes from the idea).

---

### D-01 · The keynote launch
- **World:** a humble product presented like a flagship launch: dark, quiet stage, one object huge and weighty, spec sheet, launch cadence.
- **Seen in:** Oryzo AI by Lusion (a cork coaster launched as an AI product; Awwwards Site of the Month, April 2026).
- **The scroll:** the object turns and settles as each "feature" is announced; the copy is a deadpan spec.
- **Build:** heavy in the original (Three.js); light version: a frame sequence or a few stills with scale and rotation.
- **Translate:** the client's most ordinary product, taken seriously. Needs humour that fits the brand.
- **MediaWave:** "introducing: your website" as a product launch.

### D-02 · The tiny planet
- **World:** the whole business on a small round world; walking straight ahead circles it.
- **Seen in:** Messenger by Abeto (a mail carrier on a tiny planet; an Awwwards 2025 Site of the Year winner).
- **The scroll:** the planet turns under a fixed point of view; each service is a place that rotates into view.
- **Build:** heavy in WebGL; light version: an illustrated SVG disc rotated by scroll, buildings on its rim.
- **Translate:** a business with several "places" (rooms, branches, services).
- **MediaWave:** a small planet with a web studio, an edit room, a tape shop and a photo album house.

### D-03 · Flight through floating objects
- **World:** objects hang in space; the camera flies into each one, and inside is a chapter.
- **Seen in:** Igloo Inc by Abeto and Bureaux (ice cubes, each holding a portfolio company; Awwwards Site of the Year 2024). Warning from the measurement: all text was drawn in WebGL, so search engines and screen readers got nothing, and it moved 14 MB in eight seconds. Keep the text in HTML.
- **The scroll:** a zoom-through (moves.md H-09) from object to object.
- **Build:** heavy; light version: scaled DOM objects with clip-path portals.
- **Translate:** an object the client's world is made of (crates, jars, tickets).
- **MediaWave:** flying into floating video cassettes, each holding a service.

### D-04 · Old masters gallery
- **World:** each item is a painting in a classical gallery, with modern objects painted into it.
- **Seen in:** Shopify's Renaissance Edition (150+ product updates as a scroll-driven gallery of 3D Renaissance paintings; Awwwards Site of the Day, Feb 2026, then Site of the Month).
- **The scroll:** a walk past framed works that come slightly alive (depth, light).
- **Build:** medium with commissioned or generated paintings (labelled honestly); the frames and walk are CSS.
- **Translate:** a long list (changelog, menu, services) that deserves ceremony.
- **MediaWave:** six services painted as classical scenes holding a laptop, a camera, a cassette.

### D-05 · The interactive comic
- **World:** the site is a comic: panels, gutters, speech bubbles, a hero with a goal.
- **Seen in:** Ponpon Mania by Patrick Heng and Justine Soulié (an animated web comic; Awwwards Site of the Month, Oct 2025); The New York Times' "Tomato Can Blues" (comic illustrations with parallax).
- **The scroll:** panels arrive one at a time; inside a panel, layers move a little.
- **Build:** medium; the cost is the illustration, not the code.
- **Translate:** a customer story with a before and an after.
- **MediaWave:** a business owner whose site brings nothing, a call with the developer, launch day.

### D-06 · Paintings that wake up
- **World:** still paintings or photos; under the cursor (or a long press) they start to move, and the real place appears.
- **Seen in:** David Whyte Experience by Immersive Garden (watercolours that animate under the cursor; a long press reveals the video of the real location; Site of the Day, Dec 2024).
- **The scroll:** a slow walk past stills; attention, not scroll, brings each to life.
- **Build:** medium; a poster image with a video swapped in on intent (moves.md M-01).
- **Translate:** a business that turns stills into motion, or has real places behind its pictures.
- **MediaWave:** old family photos that start to move when held: the memory-video service, shown instead of described.

### D-07 · The vanishing memorial
- **World:** a page that runs out: a timer counts, and when it ends the page fades away.
- **Seen in:** 100 Lost Species by Immersive Garden (100 species remembered in 100 seconds, then the site disappears; Site of the Day, Oct 2025).
- **The scroll:** free wandering under a running clock.
- **Build:** light to medium; the timer and fade are trivial, the restraint is not.
- **Translate:** a cause or a loss that is real and urgent (and sourced).
- **MediaWave:** tapes that fade while you watch, for the tape conversion page only, with a sourced fact.

### D-08 · The horizontal magazine
- **World:** a magazine whose spreads scroll sideways; each spread has its own layout and oversized type.
- **Seen in:** Canals of Amsterdam by Marcus Brown and Aristide Benoist (horizontal spreads, black, white and red, oversized type; images rendered in WebGL so the sideways scroll stays smooth).
- **The scroll:** one long sideways run of spreads driven by vertical scroll.
- **Build:** medium; a sticky stage with a translated track; images decoded ahead.
- **Translate:** a story with chapters, an issue, a season.
- **MediaWave:** "MediaWave, issue one": a spread per service.

### D-09 · The exploded drawing
- **World:** a technical drawing: the product comes apart into its layers, labelled with leader lines, then goes back together.
- **Seen in:** Isoduct by Mandelo (a flue duct as a 3D object that constructs and deconstructs as you scroll, instead of a spec list).
- **The scroll:** assembled, exploded, each part lit in turn, reassembled.
- **Build:** medium with CSS 3D (isometric planes), heavy with real 3D.
- **Translate:** anything made of parts the customer never sees.
- **MediaWave:** a website exploded into its layers (content, design, code, speed, a bot, video), each a service.

### D-10 · Day to night
- **World:** the page is lit by the time of day, and scrolling moves the sun: morning, noon, dusk, night.
- **Seen in:** Daylight Computer (the hero video shifts from day to night to show the screen in both lights).
- **The scroll:** the light itself is the transition; the same scene changes mood.
- **Build:** light; a fixed sky layer and CSS filters on the hero media, interpolated by scroll.
- **Translate:** a business with a day (a bakery, a gym, a clinic, a one-person studio).
- **MediaWave:** a day at the desk in the hero video, from the first coffee to the site going live at night.

### D-11 · The storybook that ends in black
- **World:** an illustrated storybook on a colourful stage, with a dramatic final page in black.
- **Seen in:** Hadaka (scroll-triggered illustrations on a colourful stage ending in a black section); Don't Board Me (an illustrated, gentle pet-care story; Site of the Day, Mar 2024).
- **The scroll:** pages turn by section; the last one changes the lighting entirely.
- **Build:** medium; illustration-led.
- **Translate:** a gentle service with an emotional turn.
- **MediaWave:** the memory-videos page: the album's pages, then the lights go down for the screening.

### D-12 · The drive down the street
- **World:** the visitor travels through the business's world, as in a game.
- **Seen in:** Bruno Simon's portfolio (drive a little car through the portfolio; Site of the Month, Jan 2026).
- **The scroll:** in the original you drive; the light version is a side-scrolling street where scroll walks you past the shop fronts.
- **Build:** heavy original; light version: SVG street in parallax layers.
- **Translate:** a local business: its street, its neighbours, its customers' shops.
- **MediaWave:** an Israeli high street where every shop sign is a site we built; our shop at the end.

### D-13 · The film foundation
- **World:** architecture as typography and frames, with a filmstrip for the work.
- **Seen in:** Siena Film Foundation by Niccolò Miranda and Federico Valla (neo-romanesque Tuscan architecture as the visual language, a WebGL filmstrip slider; Site of the Month, Mar 2025).
- **The scroll:** the filmstrip slides frame by frame; arches frame the content.
- **Build:** medium; arches are CSS masks, the strip is a sticky track.
- **Translate:** a local architecture and a medium (film, print, glass).
- **MediaWave:** Israeli Bauhaus balconies as frames, a filmstrip of work.

### D-14 · The record sleeve
- **World:** the site is an album: a sleeve, a record, side A and side B, a track list.
- **Seen in:** "Un Verano Sin Ti" (a scroll paced like a rhythm, a summer album sleeve come to life).
- **The scroll:** the record turns with scroll, the tone arm travels the grooves (progress), each track is a section.
- **Build:** light; conic-gradient grooves and a rotation.
- **Translate:** music, events, any business with a playlist of offers.
- **MediaWave:** side A websites, side B video and memories.

### D-15 · The guided tour of rooms
- **World:** a building you walk through, room by room, each room a chapter.
- **Seen in:** Hotel 1, Place Vendôme (vertical scroll plus mouse-drag through the hotel like a guided tour); Esimple (a typewriter that starts the story, then room to room).
- **The scroll:** doorways: each room is entered through the frame of the previous one.
- **Build:** medium; zoom-through portals.
- **Translate:** a physical place, or a business with clearly different "rooms".
- **MediaWave:** the studio's rooms: the desk, the edit room, the tape archive, the album shelf.

### D-16 · The archive of records
- **World:** index cards, ledgers, stamps and labels: the business as a well-kept archive.
- **Seen in:** Tracing Art by Resn for the Getty (12 million provenance records told as a continuous narrative in three chapters; Site of the Day, Jul 2025).
- **The scroll:** one record leads to the next; the archive opens drawer by drawer.
- **Build:** medium; paper, labels and type.
- **Translate:** businesses that keep things: accountants, libraries, digitising, family history.
- **MediaWave:** the shelf of labelled family tapes ("החתונה של רינה, 1994") waiting to be converted.

### D-17 · The radio tuner
- **World:** a radio: a dial scale, a needle, stations with frequencies, static between them, a tuning indicator that closes when the signal is clear.
- **Seen in:** Radio Garden (spin a globe to tune into live stations around the world), and the October 2026 MediaWave board, where Nati singled out "the frequencies and the receiver" as a world he liked.
- **The scroll:** scrolling turns the dial; between stations there is static, on a station the content comes in clear.
- **Build:** light to medium; a CSS dial, a noise layer, the needle on scroll.
- **Translate:** a business with a few clear "channels" (services, programmes, branches).
- **MediaWave:** six stations, one per service; the button is a preset key.

### D-18 · The brand's marks as a live surface
- **World:** the brand's own graphic language becomes an interactive layer over the hero.
- **Seen in:** Lando Norris by OFF+BRAND (Awwwards Site of the Year 2025: the driver's own graphic language as the interactive surface; fast, expressive transitions).
- **The scroll:** the marks react and travel between sections, carrying the identity.
- **Build:** medium; SVG marks with pointer and scroll response.
- **Translate:** a brand with a strong, ownable mark set (helmets, flags, liveries, stamps).
- **MediaWave:** needs a mark set first; not a fit until one exists.

### D-19 · The catalogue that tells a story
- **World:** a product catalogue (swatches, specs, plates) that also carries an emotional story for B2B buyers.
- **Seen in:** Floema by Bürocratik (urban furniture: catalogue and storytelling in one, Site of the Day, May 2026).
- **The scroll:** alternating plates and stories; the catalogue grid is the calm, the stories are the peaks.
- **Build:** light to medium.
- **Translate:** B2B products with real specs and real places they live in.
- **MediaWave:** less of a fit; strong for the vending-machine client (machines as catalogue plates, placements as stories).

### D-20 · The layered landscape
- **World:** an illustrated landscape in several depth layers that move at different speeds.
- **Seen in:** Firewatch (six moving layers of parallax without scroll hijacking).
- **The scroll:** the landscape slides by in depth; the sky changes at the end.
- **Build:** light to medium; the cost is the illustration.
- **Translate:** a business tied to a real landscape (a winery, a kibbutz guesthouse, a hiking guide).
- **MediaWave:** weak fit.

## Picking

- Score each candidate on: the sentence it says about this client, the real material it needs (and whether the client has it), cost on a mid-range phone, and how far it is from the client's competitors. Present three, as rendered pages (the style board), never as names.
- Never two worlds from the same family on one board (two audio devices, two galleries).
- The heavy originals (D-01, D-02, D-03, D-12) earn their place only when the brief has budget and a reason; build the light version first and judge it.
