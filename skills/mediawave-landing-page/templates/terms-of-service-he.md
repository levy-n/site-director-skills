## Section Numbering Logic

Section numbers are dynamic because conditional sections (e-commerce, disclaimers) may or may not be present:

```
Always present:
  1. קבלת התנאים
  2. תיאור האתר והשירותים
  3. קניין רוחני
  4. שימוש מותר ואסור
  5. יצירת קשר ופניות

Conditional (inserted after section 5):
  6. מסחר אלקטרוני (IF_ECOMMERCE)
  +1. הצהרה בנושא [TYPE] (business-type disclaimer)

Then always:
  +1. הגבלת אחריות
  +1. קישורים לאתרים חיצוניים
  +1. מדיניות פרטיות
  +1. שינויים בתנאי השימוש
  +1. הפרדה
  +1. דין חל וסמכות שיפוט
  +1. יצירת קשר
```

The generator should calculate actual section numbers based on which conditionals are active.

## Notes for Generator

1. Section numbers must be recalculated based on active conditional sections.
2. Only ONE business-type disclaimer should be active (food OR health OR financial OR education OR beauty).
3. The e-commerce section is independent of business-type disclaimers - both can be active.
4. The privacy policy link route (`/mediniut-pratiyut`) must match the actual Next.js route.
5. Response time default is "2 ימי עסקים" unless the business specifies differently.
6. MediaWave Israel credit in the intellectual property section is standard for all sites built through the pipeline.
7. Jurisdiction city should be the city where the business is registered/located.
8. Date format: DD בחודש YYYY (e.g., "15 במרץ 2025").
9. Keep the document concise - this is for a small business landing page, not a multinational corporation.
