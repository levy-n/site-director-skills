# Phase 4: Hebrew Copywriting & CRO

## Reference Document for MediaWave Landing Page Pipeline

This phase produces all the copy for the landing page, optimized for conversion in Hebrew. Copy is not translated from English --- it is written natively in Hebrew with Israeli cultural context.

---

## 1. StoryBrand Framework Mapping to Landing Page Sections

The StoryBrand framework (Donald Miller) maps directly to landing page structure:

### StoryBrand Elements -> Landing Page Sections

```
1. A CHARACTER (the customer)          -> Hero headline identifies WHO this is for
2. HAS A PROBLEM (external + internal) -> Pain points / problem section
3. AND MEETS A GUIDE (the brand)       -> Solution introduction + credibility
4. WHO GIVES THEM A PLAN              -> How it works / process section
5. AND CALLS THEM TO ACTION           -> CTA buttons throughout
6. THAT HELPS THEM AVOID FAILURE      -> What happens if they do nothing (urgency)
7. AND ENDS IN SUCCESS                -> Transformation / benefits / testimonials
```

### Mapping to Page Flow

```
Section 1: HERO
  - Character: Identify the visitor ("בעלי עסקים שרוצים...")
  - Problem hint: Reference their main pain in the subheadline
  - CTA: First call to action

Section 2: PROBLEM / PAIN
  - External problem: The visible, practical issue
  - Internal problem: How it makes them FEEL
  - Philosophical problem: Why this situation is WRONG

Section 3: GUIDE (Brand Introduction)
  - Empathy: "אנחנו מבינים את..."
  - Authority: credentials, experience, numbers

Section 4: THE PLAN
  - 3 simple steps (never more than 4)
  - Reduces complexity and fear
  - Each step: action + result

Section 5: FEATURES / BENEFITS
  - Feature -> Benefit translation
  - Always answer: "מה יוצא לי מזה?"

Section 6: SOCIAL PROOF
  - Testimonials (real quotes from real customers)
  - Numbers (years, clients, results)
  - Logos or trust badges

Section 7: FAILURE (What You Avoid)
  - Subtle: "without [solution], [pain continues]"
  - Not fear-mongering, but honest consequence

Section 8: SUCCESS (Transformation)
  - Paint the after picture
  - Emotional + practical outcomes

Section 9: FINAL CTA
  - Repeat the main CTA with urgency/scarcity if honest
  - Reduce friction: "ללא התחייבות", "שיחת ייעוץ חינם"

Section 10: FAQ
  - Handle remaining objections
  - Cover pricing questions, process questions, trust questions

Section 11: FOOTER
  - Contact details, legal info, social links
```

## 2. Eugene Schwartz Awareness Levels

The single most important strategic decision in copywriting is: **how aware is the visitor?** This determines everything about the headline and copy approach.

### Level 1: UNAWARE
**The visitor does not know they have a problem.**

```
Strategy:
  - Lead with a story or emotional hook
  - Do NOT mention your product or service yet
  - Agitate an emotion or curiosity

Hebrew Example (Clinic):
  ❌ "קליניקה לטיפולי שיניים" (boring, product-focused)
  ✅ "הדבר שרוב האנשים לא יודעים על כאבי הראש שלהם" (curiosity hook)

Headline Formula:
  - "[Surprising fact] + [emotional consequence]"
  - "רוב [target audience] לא יודעים ש..."

Use when:
  - Content marketing traffic (blog, social posts)
  - Cold outreach audiences
  - New/unknown problem categories
```

### Level 2: PROBLEM AWARE
**The visitor knows they have a problem but does not know solutions exist.**

```
Strategy:
  - Name and validate the problem
  - Agitate the pain --- make them feel it
  - Then introduce the solution category (NOT your brand yet)

Hebrew Example (Restaurant Marketing):
  ❌ "שירותי שיווק למסעדות"
  ✅ "המסעדה שלך ריקה בימי שלישי? זה לא בגלל האוכל."

Headline Formula:
  - "[Pain point]? יש פתרון."
  - "נמאס לך מ[pain]? הגיע הזמן ש..."
  - "[Emotional pain statement] + [bridge to hope]"

Use when:
  - SEO traffic for problem-related keywords
  - Social media ads targeting pain points
  - Referral traffic ("someone told me about this problem")
```

### Level 3: SOLUTION AWARE
**The visitor knows solutions exist but has not chosen yours yet.**

```
Strategy:
  - Differentiate immediately
  - Show WHY yours is better (unique mechanism, unique result)
  - Comparisons, credentials, proof
  - Lead with your unique value proposition

Hebrew Example (Law Firm):
  ❌ "משרד עורכי דין"
  ✅ "עורך הדין היחיד בישראל שמתמחה ב[niche] עם [X] תיקים מנוצחים"

Headline Formula:
  - "הדרך היחידה ל[result] בלי [common objection]"
  - "[Unique mechanism] שמבדיל אותנו מכל [competitor category]"
  - "למה [X] לקוחות בחרו בנו ולא ב[alternative]"

Use when:
  - Comparison shopping traffic
  - Google Ads for solution keywords
  - Competitor retargeting
```

### Level 4: PRODUCT AWARE
**The visitor knows YOUR product/service but has not bought yet.**

```
Strategy:
  - Reinforce and reassure
  - Add proof: testimonials, case studies, guarantees
  - Create urgency (honest, not fake)
  - Handle specific objections

Hebrew Example (Online Course):
  ❌ "קורס שיווק דיגיטלי"
  ✅ "367 בוגרים כבר הכפילו את ההכנסות שלהם. ההרשמה נסגרת ביום חמישי."

Headline Formula:
  - "[Social proof number] + [result] + [urgency]"
  - "[Specific objection] אנחנו יודעים. ולכן..."
  - "[Guarantee or risk reversal] + [CTA]"

Use when:
  - Retargeting campaigns (visited site before)
  - Email list campaigns (already know the brand)
  - Referrals from existing customers
```

### Level 5: MOST AWARE
**The visitor is ready to buy. They just need the offer.**

```
Strategy:
  - Minimal copy
  - Clear offer, clear price, clear CTA
  - Speed and simplicity win
  - Do NOT over-sell (it is annoying to someone ready to buy)

Hebrew Example (Service booking):
  ❌ Long sales page with 10 sections
  ✅ "קבע/י תור עכשיו | 15% הנחה להזמנה אונליין"

Headline Formula:
  - "[Product/Service] + [Offer/Discount] + [CTA]"
  - "הזמן/י עכשיו + [bonus/incentive]"
  - Just the CTA button, prominently placed

Use when:
  - Brand search traffic (searching your name)
  - Returning visitors
  - Direct links from existing customers
```

### How to Determine Awareness Level

Ask the operator via AskUserQuestion:
```
How are most visitors arriving at this landing page?

1. Cold traffic from social media or content (UNAWARE)
2. Searching for their problem on Google (PROBLEM AWARE)
3. Searching for solutions / comparing options (SOLUTION AWARE)
4. They know about the business but haven't purchased (PRODUCT AWARE)
5. Ready to buy, just need the page to convert (MOST AWARE)
6. Mixed traffic from multiple sources (we'll optimize for the primary source)
```

## 3. Hebrew Copywriting Rules

### Rule 1: WRITE in Hebrew, Do NOT Translate
- Start from scratch in Hebrew
- Think in Hebrew sentence structures
- Israeli conversational tone, not literary Hebrew
- If the source material is in English, rewrite --- never translate word-by-word

### Rule 2: Conversational, Not Formal
```
❌ Formal/Academic: "החברה שלנו מתמחה באספקת פתרונות טכנולוגיים מתקדמים"
✅ Conversational:  "אנחנו עוזרים לעסקים כמו שלך לעבוד חכם יותר עם טכנולוגיה"

❌ Stiff:    "לקבלת מידע נוסף, אנא צרו קשר עם נציגנו"
✅ Natural:  "רוצה לשמוע עוד? דבר/י איתנו"

❌ Corporate: "הארגון מתחייב למתן שירות ברמה הגבוהה ביותר"
✅ Human:     "אנחנו פשוט לא מוכנים לתת שירות בינוני"
```

### Rule 3: Short Sentences, Short Paragraphs
- Maximum 15-20 words per sentence for body copy
- Maximum 10 words for headlines
- One idea per paragraph
- Use line breaks generously
- Hebrew readers scan quickly --- make it scannable

### Rule 4: Active Voice
```
❌ Passive: "השירות ניתן על ידי צוות מומחים"
✅ Active:  "צוות המומחים שלנו נותן לך שירות אישי"

❌ Passive: "התוצאות הושגו תוך 30 יום"
✅ Active:  "הלקוחות שלנו רואים תוצאות תוך 30 יום"
```

### Rule 5: Specific, Not Vague
```
❌ Vague:    "תוצאות מעולות"
✅ Specific:  "47% גידול בלידים תוך 60 יום"

❌ Vague:    "ניסיון רב"
✅ Specific:  "12 שנות ניסיון, 340+ לקוחות מרוצים"

❌ Vague:    "מחירים אטרקטיביים"
✅ Specific:  "חבילות החל מ-₪199 לחודש"
```

## 4. Copy Structure: Section by Section

### Hero Headline
```
Formula 1: [End result] + [timeframe/ease] + [without common objection]
  "הגדל/י את המכירות ב-30% בלי לבזבז תקציב על פרסום"

Formula 2: [Target audience] + [transformation]
  "לבעלי מסעדות שרוצים שולחנות מלאים כל ערב"

Formula 3: [Pain reversal]
  "נמאס לך לרדוף אחרי לקוחות? תן/י להם לרדוף אחריך"

Formula 4: [Unique mechanism] + [result]
  "שיטת ה-3 צעדים שתהפוך את הקליניקה שלך למלאה"

Formula 5: [Social proof] + [result]
  "איך 200+ עסקים כבר מכפילים הכנסות עם [שם/שירות]"
```

### Hero Subheadline
- Expand on the headline's promise
- Add ONE specific detail (number, timeframe, method)
- 15-25 words maximum

### Pain Points Section
```
Structure:
  Section Heading: "מכיר/ה את זה?" or "נשמע מוכר?"

  Pain Point 1: [Specific, relatable scenario]
  Pain Point 2: [Another specific scenario]
  Pain Point 3: [The emotional consequence]

  Bridge: "אם זה נשמע לך מוכר, אתה/את לא לבד."
  Transition: "יש דרך אחרת."
```

### Solution Section
```
Structure:
  Heading: "[Product/Service]: הפתרון ל[problem]"

  Brief description: What it is + what it does (2-3 sentences)
  Key differentiator: Why THIS solution vs alternatives

  Benefits (not features):
    ❌ "מערכת ניהול לידים"  (feature)
    ✅ "תדע/י בדיוק מאיפה מגיע כל ליד ומה לעשות איתו" (benefit)
```

### How It Works
```
Structure:
  Heading: "איך זה עובד?" or "3 צעדים פשוטים"

  Step 1: [Action verb] + [what customer does]
    Icon + Title + 1 sentence
  Step 2: [Action verb] + [what happens]
    Icon + Title + 1 sentence
  Step 3: [Action verb] + [result they get]
    Icon + Title + 1 sentence
```

### Social Proof Section
```
Testimonial Formula:
  "[Specific result achieved]
   [How life/business changed]
   [Emotional statement about the experience]"
   - שם מלא, תפקיד/עסק, [תמונה]

Number Proof:
  [Number]+  [thing counted]
  Examples:
    "340+ לקוחות מרוצים"
    "12 שנות ניסיון"
    "98% שביעות רצון"
    "24 שעות זמן תגובה"
```

### FAQ Section
```
Always include these question types:
  1. Pricing question: "כמה זה עולה?"
  2. Process question: "איך זה עובד?"
  3. Time question: "כמה זמן לוקח?"
  4. Trust question: "מה אם זה לא מתאים לי?"
  5. Comparison question: "למה לבחור בכם?"
  6. Technical question: (industry-specific)
```

## 5. CTA Button Copy

### Hebrew CTA Rules

**Use Specific Action Verbs (Not Generic)**
```
❌ Generic:  "שלח" / "לחץ כאן" / "המשך"
✅ Specific: "קבע/י תור" / "הורד/י את המדריך" / "שלח/י הודעה"
```

**CTA Examples by Industry**

| Industry | Primary CTA | Secondary CTA |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| Restaurant | "הזמן/י שולחן" | "ראה/י תפריט" |
| Medical | "קבע/י תור" | "שלח/י שאלה" |
| Legal | "שלח/י פנייה" | "חייג/י עכשיו" |
| Salon | "קבע/י תור" | "ראה/י מחירון" |
| Course | "הרשם/י עכשיו" | "הורד/י סילבוס" |
| SaaS | "התחל/י בחינם" | "ראה/י דמו" |
| Fitness | "שיעור ניסיון חינם" | "ראה/י מחירים" |
| Real Estate | "תאם/י סיור" | "שלח/י פרטים" |
| Agency | "קבל/י הצעת מחיר" | "ראה/י עבודות" |

### Gendered Hebrew in CTAs

**Option 1: Slash Format (Safe Default)**
```
"שלח/י הודעה"
"הזמן/י עכשיו"
"קבע/י תור"
"הרשם/י לניוזלטר"
```

**Option 2: Target Audience Gender (When Known)**
```
Female target (salon, jewelry): "קבעי תור" / "הזמיני עכשיו"
Male target (barbershop, auto): "קבע תור" / "שלח הודעה"
Mixed: use slash format or infinitive ("להזמין עכשיו")
```

**Option 3: Infinitive Form (Gender Neutral)**
```
"להזמין עכשיו"
"לקבוע תור"
"לקבל הצעה"
```

### CTA Button Pairing
```
Primary CTA:    Strong color (primary or accent), full background
  "קבע/י תור עכשיו"

Secondary CTA:  Ghost/outline style, softer
  "רוצה לשמוע עוד?"  or  "חייג/י אלינו"
```

## 6. Message Match

### The Rule
The landing page copy MUST match the ad, email, or social post that drives traffic to it.

```
If the ad says:     "הזמן ארוחה זוגית ב-₪199"
The page must say:  "ארוחה זוגית ב-₪199" in the hero
NOT:                "ברוכים הבאים למסעדת..." (disconnect)

If the email says:  "5 טעויות שהורסות את השיווק שלך"
The page must say:  "5 הטעויות שהורסות את השיווק שלך" + expand on them
NOT:                "שירותי שיווק דיגיטלי" (disconnect)
```

### Message Match Checklist
- [ ] Headline uses the same language/keywords as the traffic source
- [ ] Same offer mentioned in the ad appears above the fold
- [ ] Visual style matches the ad creative (colors, mood)
- [ ] CTA matches the ad promise
- [ ] No bait-and-switch: what was promised is what is delivered

### Ask the Operator
```
What traffic sources will drive visitors to this landing page?
1. Google Ads (what keywords?)
2. Facebook/Instagram ads (what's the ad copy/angle?)
3. Email campaigns (what's the email subject/hook?)
4. Organic social media
5. Direct/referral traffic
6. Other: ___

This helps me match the landing page copy to what visitors already saw.
```

## 7. Page-Level CRO Principles

### Single CTA Per Page
- One primary conversion action for the entire page
- Every CTA button leads to the SAME action (form, phone, booking)
- Do NOT split attention between "buy this" AND "sign up for that" AND "download this"
- Secondary CTAs (ghost buttons) should support the primary (e.g., "learn more" scrolls to info, then primary CTA)

**Statistic:** Single-CTA pages convert at 13.5% vs 2-3% for pages with multiple competing CTAs.

### Personalized CTAs
- Address the visitor directly: "שלך", "שלי", "אני רוצה"
- **Statistic:** Personalized CTAs perform 202% better than generic ones

```
❌ Generic:      "שלח טופס"
✅ Personalized: "אני רוצה לקבל הצעת מחיר"

❌ Generic:      "הרשמה"
✅ Personalized: "אני רוצה להתחיל"
```

### Social Proof Placement
- Place social proof IMMEDIATELY after the first CTA (hero section)
- Place testimonials near the bottom CTA (before final conversion push)
- Numbers/stats can appear early (builds credibility before reading details)

### Urgency Without Being Sleazy
```
✅ Honest Urgency:
  - "נשארו 3 מקומות אחרונים" (if true)
  - "המחיר עולה ב-1 לינואר" (if true)
  - "ההרשמה נסגרת ביום חמישי" (if true)

❌ Fake/Sleazy:
  - Fake countdown timers that reset
  - "רק היום!" (every day)
  - "מכירה אחרונה!" (every week)
```

### Friction Reduction
After every CTA, add a friction reducer:
```
"ללא התחייבות"
"שיחת ייעוץ ראשונה חינם"
"ניתן לבטל בכל עת"
"תשובה תוך 24 שעות"
"100% דיסקרטי"
```

## 10. Tier 3 Copy Validation (AskUserQuestion Gates)

Before finalizing copy, present these validation questions to the operator:

### Gate 1: Headline Validation
```
I've written 3 headline options for [Client Name]:

1. "[Headline A]"
   Strategy: [Why this works]

2. "[Headline B]"
   Strategy: [Why this works]

3. "[Headline C]"
   Strategy: [Why this works]

Which resonates most with how your client talks about their business?
Feel free to mix, modify, or reject all and I'll write more.
```

### Gate 2: Customer Language Check
```
I want to make sure the copy sounds like YOUR customers actually talk.

Can you share:
1. Common phrases customers use when describing their problem?
2. Words the client uses to describe their service?
3. Any specific terminology from this industry I should use (or avoid)?

This helps the copy feel authentic, not generic.
```

### Gate 3: Objection Handling
```
What are the top 3-5 objections or hesitations customers have before buying?

For example:
- "It's too expensive"
- "I'm not sure it will work for me"
- "I had a bad experience elsewhere"

I'll address each one in the copy and FAQ section.
```

### Gate 4: Best Customer Quote
```
Does the client have a favorite customer quote, review, or testimonial?

The BEST social proof is a real quote from a real customer that says:
1. What problem they had
2. What solution they tried
3. What result they got

Even a Google review screenshot works. Real words beat written copy every time.
```

### Gate 5: CTA Button Text Validation
```
For the main call-to-action button, I'm recommending:

Primary CTA: "[Button text]"
  - Appears: [X] times on the page
  - Action: [what happens when clicked]

Secondary CTA: "[Button text]"
  - Appears: [X] times on the page
  - Action: [what happens when clicked]

Does this feel right for the client's audience?
Should the tone be more formal / casual / urgent?
```
